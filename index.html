GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently asked questions.

What is Harvestify?

Harvestify is being designed to help investors explore potential stock-sale scenarios and understand their possible financial impact.

How does the sale simulator work?

The sample demo uses quantity, purchase price and selling price to calculate an illustrative capital gain before charges and adjustments.

Does the demo calculate my actual tax?

No. The current sample demo does not calculate actual tax. A complete estimate would require transaction details and applicable tax rules.

What is FIFO?

FIFO means First In, First Out. In a simplified share-sale example, the earliest purchased eligible shares are treated as sold first.

Does Harvestify place sell orders?

This website demo does not place orders or connect to a broker. It is for exploring sample calculations only.
